§Module boundary::dirichlet

Dirichlet boundary condition on a boundary region of the mesh (#202).

§Condition

Every node of the region holds a prescribed value, constant or given as a function of time:

$$u = g(t) \quad \text{on the region}$$

The time is read from ComputeContext::time; the condition requires no context variable.

§Region

The condition acts on a BoundaryRegion the mesh resolves with Mesh::boundary_nodes (DA-071): with the default implementation of Mesh, either face of a 1D mesh, normal to axis 0. A mesh that does not define the region refuses it with PreconditionFailed. Only the nodes of the region are needed, not their interior neighbours: unlike the Danckwerts conditions, a Dirichlet condition also applies on a 1D mesh of a single node.

§Constrained nodes (DD-070)

The nodes of the region are declared through BoundaryCondition::constrained_nodes: the solver keeps their derivative at zero and the solver drivers re-apply the condition to the initial state and at the end of each step, at the exact time of the saved state. Every saved state holds g(t) at those nodes, bit for bit.

§Location

BoundaryCondition::location keeps its default, None: the region already designates where the condition acts, and a BoundaryLocation (Inlet, Wall…) carries a physical role a held value does not imply.

§Serialisation

Not serialisable: the time-dependent value is a closure. A configuration that needs persistence rebuilds the condition from its own description, as BoundaryCondition recommends.
